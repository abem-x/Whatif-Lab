How it works

We model a projectile launched from and landing at the same height with no air resistance. The motion is split into horizontal and vertical parts.

  • x(t) = v₀ cos(θ) · t
  • y(t) = v₀ sin(θ) · t − ½gt²
  • R = v₀² sin(2θ) / g

This is a simplified school model that neglects air resistance and drag.

Neutral

A pH of 7 is considered neutral on this simplified educational scale.

  • pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]
  • Lower pH = more acidic
  • Higher pH = more basic

pH describes the balance of hydrogen ions in a solution; it does not identify every chemical or hazard by itself.
